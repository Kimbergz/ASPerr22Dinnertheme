Privacy Policy

Malam Inspirasi ASPutra 2027: Theme Vote · Last updated 23 November 2026

This website lets ASPer22 students at Universiti Putra Malaysia vote for the theme of the Malam Inspirasi ASPutra 2027 batch dinner. It is run by the Majlis Tertinggi Persatuan Pelajar ASPer Sesi 2026/2027 (HiCom ASPer22).

What we collect

  • Your UPM student email address, from signing in with Google. We use it to read your matric number and confirm you are on the ASPer22 batch list.
  • The theme you vote for and the time you voted.
  • Your name and class, which come from the batch list HiCom already holds, not from your Google account.

We do not access your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or any other Google data. The sign-in only shares your name and email address.

Why we collect it

Only to make sure each ASPer22 student votes once, and to count the votes fairly. Your information is not used for anything else, and it is never sold or shared outside HiCom.

Who can see it

Only HiCom ASPer22 members running the vote can see individual votes. The website itself shows no names or individual votes. Results are announced by HiCom.

Where it is stored and for how long

Votes are stored in a secure database hosted by Supabase. They are kept until the dinner theme is confirmed in the programme proposal (kertas kerja), and then deleted, no later than the end of the Malam Inspirasi ASPutra 2027 event.

Cookies

The site keeps your sign-in only for the current browser tab and signs you out right after you vote. It also remembers whether you muted the sound. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

Voting is voluntary. If you would like your vote record removed, or have any question about this policy, contact HiCom ASPer22 through your class representative.
